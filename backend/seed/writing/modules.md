# Modules added by signals

| Module | Added when | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data handling | Compliance signal present | One sentence on access control, encryption and audit logging, naming their standard (HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2, PCI) |
| Staged autonomy | AI acts on the client's behalf | AI drafts or recommends first, a person approves, then selected actions are automated once proven |
| Integration first look | Integration risk (EHR, ERP, CRM or legacy system named) | One sentence on confirming what their system exposes before committing |
| Quote rule | Pricing requested | An honest line that an accurate estimate needs a short call, plus the offer of one. Also listed under "Needs your eye" |
| Team backup | Who they want is "either" or "team" | One line that the team can add capacity |
| Forwardable summary | Decision maker is a middleman | One plain line summarizing the approach that can be forwarded |
| Volume line | Data and scale stated | One line on handling their stated volume |

Each module is one or two sentences, placed where it reads naturally, never as a separate heading.

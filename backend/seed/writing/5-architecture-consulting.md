# Type 5. Architecture or consulting

**Chosen when:** architect, lead, consultant or auditor role, or the job asks for strategy, assessment or a roadmap.
**Length:** 160 to 240 words.

## Structure

1. **Hook.** A point of view on the key decision they face.
2. **Proof.** Decisions we owned and why, not features we built.
3. **Deliverables.** What they will have at the end: architecture, assessment, roadmap, standards.
4. **Close.** An offer to start with a short discovery session.
5. **Signature** from the profile record.

Voice shifts from "I built" to "I owned" and "I decided", only where true.

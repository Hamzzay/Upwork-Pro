# Screening answers

* Written separately from the cover letter, one per question, in the client's order.
* Each opens with a direct answer: yes, no, or the fact asked for.
* Then one or two sentences of proof, only from the project library and the profile record.
* The same rules on numbers and banned phrases apply.
* A question that cannot be answered honestly (years in a language Stackup does not use, a system never built) is flagged under "Needs your eye", never padded.
* Certification questions follow the rule below.
* Pricing or estimate questions use the estimate answer rule in `modules.md`.
* Keep each answer under 80 words unless the question asks for detail.

## Certification questions

Only when the client explicitly asks for a certification: a screening question about certifications, or a certification the post lists as required. Never mention certifications otherwise.

1. **Use only certifications on the sending profile** (Certifications in the profile record). Never choose, suggest or invent a certification the profile does not list, even if it is likely the person holds it.
2. **Pick the listed certification that answers the question best:** the cloud, tool, compliance standard or role the client named. Write it as "Certification name, Issuer, year".
3. **One to two sentences:** the certification, then one line of proof tying it to a library project or to the tool the job names.
4. **No listed certification matches:** do not claim one, and do not answer with a flat negative either. Answer with the strongest true credentials (hands on projects with the named tool, compliance work delivered, platform status) and list it under "Needs your eye" as "No matching certification on this profile."

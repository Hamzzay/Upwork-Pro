# Type 3. Invite

**Chosen when:** the client invited the profile.
**Length:** 100 to 160 words.

## Structure

1. **Greeting.** "Hi [name], thanks for the invite." If no name is given, go straight to the hook.
2. **Hook.** One specific observation about their post.
3. **Proof.** One project, closely matched. Link on its own line.
4. **Close.** A concrete next step, since they already chose to look at us: a short call, or the first piece of work.
5. **Signature** from the profile record.

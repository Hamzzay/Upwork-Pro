# Type 2. Structured submission

**Chosen when:** the client lists what proposals must include. Wins over every other type.
**Length:** as long as the answers need, no padding.

## Structure

1. **One line hook** tied to their most important requirement. If the client set a required opening, use it exactly instead.
2. **Answers,** numbered in their order, using their wording as short headings. Proof goes inside the answers.
3. **Gaps.** If an item cannot be answered honestly, do not pad it. List it under "Needs your eye".
4. **Close.** One specific question.
5. **Signature** from the profile record.

Pricing asks follow the quote rule module. Everything else is answered in full.

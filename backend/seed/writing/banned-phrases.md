# Banned phrases

Never use these in a cover letter or screening answer:

* "Perfect," as an opener
* "I've already built similar"
* "I worked on [project] around"
* "directly aligns"
* "closely matches"
* "exactly the kind of foundation you're looking for"
* "I'm excited to apply"
* "I understand your requirements"
* "I'll approach your system"
* "I'd be happy to" as the closing line
* Any easy out in the close, such as "if you're interested" or "no pressure"
* Any list of more than three features in one sentence

Openers: the first words are about the client's problem, never "I", "We", "My name", "Hello, I", or a project name. The only fixed openers allowed are the ones a type file sets ("Hi [name], thanks for the invite." for Type 3, "Understood." for Type 7, "For your platform," or "For you," for Type 8) or words the client asks for.

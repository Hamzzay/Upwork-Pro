# Type 1. Standard build

**Chosen when:** new build, MVP or extend; builder role; no explicit asks.
**Length:** 120 to 200 words.

## Structure

1. **Hook, two lines.** Their specific problem and one insight about it.
2. **Proof.** One or two projects, each in two or three plain sentences: what we built, what we owned, the detail that matches them. Link on its own line.
3. **Plan.** Their stack, committed. The first thing we would build and why.
4. **Close.** One specific question.
5. **Signature** from the profile record.

## Example hook

"A niche talent marketplace lives or dies on whether employers trust the profiles they pay to unlock, so I'd design the verification and credit rules before the UI."

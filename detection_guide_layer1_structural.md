# DETECTION GUIDE — LAYER 1: STRUCTURAL SIGNALS (v2)
### Each signal has: what it decides, how to detect each STATED value, the proposal move per value, and a defined FALLBACK move for when the post says nothing. The fallback is a real instruction, never a blank.

---

## SIGNAL 1 — WHO THEY WANT (Agency / Small team / Solo / [Fallback])

**Decides:** whether to front a team or a single named lead engineer.

**AGENCY/TEAM — detect:** "agency", "small team", "development/engineering team", "dev shop", "capacity", "bandwidth", company-level references/profile requested; or scope one person plainly can't deliver.
- *Move:* present Stackup as a team with a named lead; emphasise capacity and coverage across roles.

**SOLO/INDIVIDUAL — detect:** "independent contractor", "freelancer", "individual", "one person", "work autonomously", "self-directed"; first-person "I want someone who", availability/hours questions.
- *Move:* front ONE named senior engineer; personal ownership, direct line to you.

**BOTH ("developer or small team") — detect:** names both explicitly.
- *Move:* lead with a named person, mention team depth as backup.

**FALLBACK (nothing stated — this is 51% of posts):** front a named senior lead engineer AND state a team stands behind them. This wins solo-leaning posts without losing team-leaning ones. Never guess "solo" or "agency" from silence.

---

## SIGNAL 2 — ENGAGEMENT MODEL (Fixed / Hourly-ongoing / Discovery-first / [Fallback])

**Decides:** the commercial framing of the proposal.

**FIXED — detect:** "fixed price", "milestone-based", "one number", stated total budget + defined deliverable.
- *Move:* give a confident fixed structure with clear milestones.

**HOURLY/ONGOING — detect:** "/hr", "hourly", "hrs/week", "ongoing", "retainer", "long-term", duration fields ("1 to 3 months"), "maintenance".
- *Move:* propose a rate + an ongoing engagement; emphasise you stick around post-launch.

**DISCOVERY-FIRST — detect:** "discovery phase", "workflow assessment", "architecture phase", "independent estimate", "scope before budget".
- *Move:* lead with a small paid discovery step as the entry point.

**FALLBACK (nothing stated):** propose the engagement structure YOU recommend for the work described (usually a short discovery step, then ongoing), framed as best practice. Lead the framing rather than matching a signal that isn't there.

---

## SIGNAL 3 — PROJECT MATURITY (Greenfield / Rescue-extend / [Fallback])

**Decides:** whether the proposal opens with a build plan or a diagnosis.

**GREENFIELD/MVP — detect:** "from scratch", "greenfield", "new product", "build the MVP", no mention of any existing system.
- *Move:* open with a confident build path and a clear MVP scope.

**RESCUE/EXTEND — detect:** "not a greenfield project", "already have", "existing", "previous/last developer", "take over", "migrate", "broke", "not working", "inspect", "refactor".
- *Move:* open with diagnosis — "rebuilds like this fail because X, here's how I avoid it."

**FALLBACK (nothing stated — 55% of posts):** open with ONE light diagnostic line that works either way ("First thing I'd do is confirm what's already in place before touching anything"). This reads as senior on a greenfield post AND correct on a rescue, so you never assume wrong.

---

## SIGNAL 4 — POSITIONING AXIS (Technical / Product-outcome / [Fallback])

**Decides:** whether the voice leads with architecture or business outcome.

**TECHNICAL — detect:** "architecture", "infrastructure", "pipeline", "backend", "multi-tenant", "RAG", "vector", "senior engineer", "hands-on".
- *Move:* lead with senior technical depth and how it's built.

**PRODUCT/OUTCOME — detect:** "client-facing", "own the relationship", "consultant", "strategy", "outcomes", "go-live", "discovery with leadership".
- *Move:* lead with business results and ownership, prove technical depth after.

**FALLBACK (nothing stated):** default to TECHNICAL voice (58% of the pipeline is technical, only 2% purely product), but include one outcome sentence early so a product-minded reader is covered.

---

## SIGNAL 5 — STRUCTURED SUBMISSION DEMANDED (Yes / No)

**Decides:** whether format compliance is a pass/fail gate. (No fallback needed — this signal is binary and always readable.)

**YES — detect:** "generic proposals are not read", "answer the following", "include the word X", numbered required sections, mandatory attachments.
- *Move:* follow the structure EXACTLY, first, before anything else. Deviation = auto-reject.

**NO — detect:** open-ended "share your experience", no structure specified.
- *Move:* use your own strongest structure.

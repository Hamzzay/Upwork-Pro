# DETECTION GUIDE — LAYER 3: HIDDEN SIGNALS (v2)
### The read-between-the-lines layer. NOT keyword-matchable — the phrases below raise probability, they don't prove. Each signal has its stated tells, the proposal move, and a defined FALLBACK for when the post gives no tone signal (the majority case here).

IMPORTANT: for every hidden signal, "neutral/no tell" is the MOST common outcome. So the fallback is not an edge case — it is the default path the system takes most of the time. Make it strong.

---

## SIGNAL 11 — PAIN BEHIND THE POST

**RESCUE/FRUSTRATION — detect:** "last/previous developer", "not working", "broke", "failed", "done right this time", "unreliable", over-specifying what NOT to do.
- *Move:* name the pain in the first line; open with diagnosis and reassurance.

**FRESH/OPPORTUNITY — detect:** excitement, "building", "new product", no past-pain mention.
- *Move:* lead with the vision and a confident path.

**FALLBACK (neutral — 80%):** open with quiet competence — a specific, concrete observation about their actual project that proves you read it. No manufactured empathy, no assumed pain. Specificity IS the hook when there's no emotional tell.

---

## SIGNAL 12 — DECISION-MAKER vs MIDDLEMAN

**FOUNDER — detect:** "I want", "my company", "our business", "we are a [business]", emotional stake.
- *Move:* speak to their business outcome, reassure the person.

**MIDDLEMAN — detect:** "our client", "on behalf of", "do not contact", white-label, staffing/agency framing.
- *Move:* make THEM the hero to their client; emphasise you are low-risk and easy to manage.

**FALLBACK (unclear — 53%, and hides most real middlemen):** write to the person as if they are the owner, BUT keep it easy to forward — professional, low-drama, no over-familiarity. This covers both a quiet founder and a hidden middleman without betting on either.

---

## SIGNAL 13 — PRICE vs VALUE ANCHOR

**VALUE — detect:** "most experienced", "senior", "expert", "willing to pay", "quality", "actually shipped", "proven".
- *Move:* lead with depth and senior proof; do not discount.

**PRICE — detect:** "affordable", "tight budget", "cheap", "low maintenance" (note: low-maintenance can be pragmatic-quality, a good buyer).
- *Move:* decide compete-or-skip; usually skip pure price buyers.

**FALLBACK (neutral — 35%):** assume value-leaning (only ~1% are openly price-driven). Lead with quality and seniority by default; never open by competing on price.

---

## SIGNAL 14 — CLARITY OF THEIR OWN THINKING

**PRECISE — detect:** numbered scope, deliverables, explicit tech choices, "must include", defined constraints.
- *Move:* CONFIRM understanding and show exact execution; don't re-explain their own spec.

**VAGUE — detect:** "figure out", "not sure", "open to ideas", "help us", "explore", "identify where".
- *Move:* LEAD — show the path, propose discovery, turn their fog into a plan.

**FALLBACK (neutral — 67%):** take gentle leadership. Restate their goal in one crisp sentence to show understanding, then propose a clear first step. Works whether they were secretly precise or secretly vague.

---

## SIGNAL 15 — TRUST TEMPERATURE

**GUARDED/BURNED — detect:** "generic proposals not read", "disqualified if", "do not contact", strict rules, NDAs, long requirement lists, "must".
- *Move:* acknowledge their rules, follow them visibly, sell reliability/process BEFORE skill.

**OPEN — detect:** casual, trusting, "open to suggestions", few rules.
- *Move:* respond with warmth and ideas.

**FALLBACK (no strong tell):** default to calm, professional, process-aware — show you are safe to hire without being defensive. Neutral-professional never alienates an open client and reassures a quietly guarded one.

---

## SIGNAL 16 — BUYING URGENCY & INTENT

**READY — detect:** "start immediately", "ASAP", "this week", fixed near-term deadline, "hiring now".
- *Move:* close with confidence and a concrete fast start plan.

**NURTURE — detect:** "budget discussed later", "evaluating", "exploring", "getting estimates", "down the road".
- *Move:* offer a low-friction next step (call/discovery); don't hard-close.

**FALLBACK (neutral — 92%, i.e. almost always):** Urgency is essentially unreadable from Upwork text, so do NOT infer it from the description. Default to a confident-but-not-pushy close: one clear next step, no false urgency. Pull real urgency from job age / invites / client activity elsewhere, not from this layer.

---

## OVERALL RULE
Score each hidden signal as a probability. When two or more point the same way (rescue pain + guarded + value), that combination dominates the voice. When ALL are neutral — the common case — fall back to: specific, calm, senior, one clear next step. That neutral-default voice is the backbone the system uses most often, so it must be strong on its own.

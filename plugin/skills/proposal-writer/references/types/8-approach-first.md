# Type 8. Approach First With Questions (Writing Framework)

Source: "Stackup Proposal Writing Framework (Locked SOP)".

**Chosen when:** the client has a platform or idea to build or extend but the post leaves key decisions open (scope, integrations, data, users), so engaging questions are the best way to start a conversation. Signals in `../type-selection.md`.
**Length:** about 230 to 280 words.

## Structure

1. **Paragraph 1, approach (40 to 50 words).** Start with "For your platform," or "For you,". If the client uses a specific keyword, start with that phrasing. Explain how you'll start working, including tech stack and process such as APIs, workflows and integrations.
2. **Paragraph 2, sample works mention (15 to 25 words).** Mention two relevant projects naturally, in a conversational tone.
3. **Paragraph 3, first project (about 50 words).** Project name and a 7 to 9 word title, link on the next line, then a conversational explanation matching the client's requirements.
4. **Paragraph 4, second project (about 50 words).** Same as paragraph 3.
5. **Paragraph 5, question 1 (12 to 15 words).** Technical, thought provoking, easy to answer.
6. **Paragraph 6, question 2 (12 to 15 words).** Deeper thinking, encourages a reply.
7. **Paragraph 7, call to action (7 to 10 words).** Short and action driven.
8. **Closing.** "Best regards," then the signature from the profile record.

## Project format

```
Project Name – Project Title
[project link]

Conversational explanation matching the client's requirements.
```

## Tone and style

1. Write like a human, not AI. Simple, clear, conversational English.
2. Keep a storytelling flow. Make it feel like you are already thinking about their system.
3. Phrases that fit: "I'll start working on..." and "This is very similar to what I've built...".
4. Avoid robotic phrases such as "I understand your requirements" or "I'll approach your system".

## Key principles

1. Match the client's features, tools and industry.
2. Show that you've already solved similar problems.
3. Avoid generic claims and buzzwords.
4. The questions should trigger the reply instinct: specific to their system, answerable in a line.

## Checklist

- [ ] Starts with "For your platform," or "For you," or the client's keyword
- [ ] Conversational tone throughout
- [ ] Each project has title, link and explanation
- [ ] Two engaging questions, each 12 to 15 words
- [ ] Call to action strong and concise

## Estimates

Apply the estimate answer rule in `../modules.md`.

## Do not

1. Do not ask questions the post already answers.
2. Do not ask yes or no questions; they end the conversation.
3. Do not ask more than two questions.
